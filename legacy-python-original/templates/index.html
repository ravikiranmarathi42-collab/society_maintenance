Key Capabilities

Smart Modules Built For Society Operations

Everything management committees and apartment residents need in one unified, responsive web portal.

Flat & Resident Directory

Track all residential units, blocks, floor numbers, occupancy statuses (Occupied / Vacant), and resident profiles securely.

Maintenance Billing

Automate monthly maintenance bill generation for all flats with customizable rates, due dates, and overdue penalty calculations.

Payments & Receipts

Simulate seamless online payments via UPI, Net Banking, and Cards. Generate printable, branded society payment receipts immediately.

Helpdesk & Complaints

Residents log maintenance issues (plumbing, electrical, security, lift). Administrators review, assign technicians, and track resolution.

Notices & Circulars

Broadcast AGM notices, water shutdown alerts, festive celebrations, and society rules directly to all resident dashboards.

Expenses & Audit Reports

Record day-to-day expenditures (security, electricity, gardening, repairs). Generate income-expense sheets and export reports to CSV.

About The Project

Modern Housing Society Governance Made Simple

Managing a modern residential complex requires continuous coordination between the managing committee, accounting staff, facility workers, and apartment residents. The Society Maintenance Management System digitizes conventional paper ledgers, eliminates collection delays, and provides complete accountability.
